Simulated Annealing

Three live examples of the SimulatedAnnealing Elm package, each chosen to exercise a different part of the library.

  • Traveling Salesman

    Cities placed randomly on a canvas; a tour is annealed in front of you using 2-opt segment reversals, untangling as the temperature drops.

  • Time Windows

    A delivery route with per-stop opening hours. The energy function walks the tour in order, carrying an accumulated clock forward so waiting for a city to open is penalized.

  • Sudoku

    Pure constraint satisfaction with no notion of distance. Compares live annealing, the calibrated scheme from Rhyd Lewis's 2007 paper, and a plain backtracking solver on the same puzzle.
